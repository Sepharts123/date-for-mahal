For the one I love

My Love,

Happy Monthsary, lovey. ♡

I don't think I will ever get tired of saying how thankful I am that you came into my life. Out of all the people in this world, somehow our paths found each other—and that is one of the most beautiful things that ever happened to me.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for understanding me, even on the days when I am difficult to understand. Thank you for your patience, your loyalty, your little efforts, your kindness, and for loving me in ways that sometimes I don't even know how to ask for.

I know we are not perfect. We have our misunderstandings, quiet days, busy days, and moments when things don't go the way we want them to. But even through all of that, my heart still chooses you.

You deserve a love that feels safe. A love that stays. A love that chooses you again and again.

And I want to be that person for you.

If I could give you one thing in this life, I would give you the ability to see yourself through my eyes—so you could finally understand how precious, beautiful, and deserving of love you are.

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for understanding me. Thank you for your loyalty. Thank you for being you.

I gave you the love I know how to give, but every day, I still want to learn how to love you better.

Forever choosing you,your lovey ♡
